Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about booking, packages and what to expect on the day.

Booking

How do I check if my date is available?

Fill in the form on the Book a date page or message us on Messenger with your event date, venue and estimated guest count. We’ll reply with availability and a quote.

How early should I book?

As early as you can. Dates fill up fast, especially for weddings, debuts and holiday-season events. Once you know your date and venue, send us a message.

What details do you need for a quote?

Your event type, date, venue, rough guest count and which acts you’d like: host, magician, sound and lights, or all three.

Services

Can I book just the host, just the magician, or just the sound and lights?

Yes. Every act can be booked on its own. Many clients combine them, and Host + Magic is our most booked package.

What kinds of events do you do?

Birthdays, weddings, debuts, corporate events, christenings, graduations, fiestas and launches. If your celebration isn’t on the list, ask us.

What languages does the host use?

English, Filipino and Bisaya. Tell us your crowd and we’ll match the program.

Is the magic suitable for kids and adults?

Yes. We offer walk-around close-up magic and stage shows for kids or adults, with comedy and mentalism available, plus custom tricks for your celebrant.

Sound & lights

Do you bring your own equipment?

Yes. DeDios Audio and Lights brings the sound system, wireless mics and lighting, sized to your venue and guest count.

Is there someone to run the equipment?

Yes. An on-site tech operator runs sound and lights for the whole event, and handles setup, sound check and teardown.

What size events can you cover?

From intimate home gatherings of up to 50 guests, to parties of 50 to 150, to grand events of 150+ in ballrooms, stages and big corporate shows.

Pricing & payment

How much does it cost?

Every event is different, so we quote to fit your date, venue, guest count and the acts you choose. Send us your details and we’ll reply with a clear price.

Do you travel outside Negros Occidental?

Our home base is Negros Occidental. For events elsewhere, message us your venue and we’ll let you know if we can make it work.

How do I confirm my booking?

After you accept the quote, we’ll confirm the date and the payment details with you directly. Message us on Messenger or by phone if you have any questions along the way.

Still curious?

Ask us anything.

Tell us about your event and we’ll reply with answers, availability and a quote.
